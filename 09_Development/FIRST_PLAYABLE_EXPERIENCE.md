# First Playable Experience

## Purpose

This document defines the first experience a player has when starting DROPi Tycoon Prototype v0.1.

The objective is to create a simple but meaningful introduction to the core gameplay loop.

The player should quickly understand:

- What the game is about
- What actions they can take
- How the company grows

---

# Player Fantasy

The player starts as a small logistics entrepreneur.

They do not own a large company.

They begin with:

- Limited resources
- Basic equipment
- A small operating area

The goal is to grow from a small delivery service into a logistics empire.

---

# First Launch Experience

## Main Menu

The player sees:

- Game title
- Start Game button
- Settings
- Information

The interface should be simple and mobile friendly.

---

# Starting Situation

The player begins with:

## Company

Name:

Player chooses company name.

---

## Resources

Initial state:

- Small amount of money
- Basic delivery capability
- One operating zone

---

## World

The first map contains:

- Small neighborhood
- Several buildings
- Customers
- Delivery destinations

The world should feel alive but remain simple.

---

# First Tutorial Sequence

The tutorial teaches through actions.

No long explanations.

---

# Step 1: Receive First Order

The player receives a simple delivery request.

Example:

Customer:

Local restaurant

Request:

Deliver package to nearby customer.

Information shown:

- Pickup location
- Destination
- Reward

---

# Step 2: Choose Delivery Method

The player selects how to complete the delivery.

Prototype options:

- Walk delivery

Future options:

- Bicycle
- Vehicle
- Drone

---

# Step 3: Complete Delivery

The player reaches the destination.

The game provides feedback:

- Delivery completed
- Reward received
- Customer satisfaction increased

---

# Step 4: First Reward

The player receives:

- Money
- Experience
- Company reputation

The reward explains progression.

---

# Step 5: First Upgrade

The player receives the first improvement opportunity.

Example:

Upgrade:

Better delivery efficiency

Effect:

- Faster deliveries
- Higher income potential

---

# Core Emotional Moment

The first important feeling should be:

"I started with nothing and I improved my company."

The player should understand that every delivery creates progress.

---

# First 5 Minutes Goal

After five minutes, the player should understand:

- How deliveries work
- How money is earned
- How upgrades work
- Why growing the company matters

---

# Prototype Limitations

The first experience does not include:

- Drone systems
- Large cities
- Complex economy
- Advanced AI
- Multiplayer

---

# Design Rules

The first experience must be:

- Simple
- Fast
- Rewarding
- Understandable

Avoid:

- Complex menus
- Too many choices
- Advanced mechanics too early

---

# Success Criteria

The first playable experience is successful if a new player can:

1. Start the game.
2. Understand the objective.
3. Complete a delivery.
4. Receive a reward.
5. Want to continue growing the company.

---

# Canonical Rule

The first five minutes define the player's relationship with DROPi Tycoon.

The beginning must create curiosity, understanding, and motivation.
