# Game Balancing Rules

## Purpose

This document defines the balancing principles for DROPi Tycoon Prototype v0.1.

The goal is to create a progression experience where the player feels constant improvement while maintaining meaningful decisions.

---

## Scope and Ownership

Balancing rules in this document are Prototype v0.1 scope constraints. They narrow, not redefine, the canonical rules owned by `01_GameDesign/PROGRESSION.md`, `01_GameDesign/GAMEPLAY.md`, `02_Economy/ECONOMY.md`, and `03_Logistics/ORDERS.md`.

Canonical game design authority remains with those domain documents. This document does not independently own economy rules, progression rules, logistics rules, reputation rules, or balancing philosophy.

---

# Balancing Philosophy

Derived from canonical game design principles in `01_GameDesign/GDD.md` and `01_GameDesign/PROGRESSION.md`.

The game should create the feeling:

"Every decision helps me build a better company."

The player should:

- Progress steadily
- Understand rewards
- Make strategic choices
- Feel motivated to continue

---

# Core Balance Principles

## Reward Progress

Successful actions should provide visible progress.

Example:

Complete delivery

↓

Earn money

↓

Buy improvement

↓

Complete better deliveries

---

## Avoid Extreme Progression

The game should avoid:

Too fast:

- Everything unlocked immediately
- No meaningful choices

Too slow:

- Long waiting periods
- Repetitive actions without reward

---

# Economy Balance

## Income Sources

Prototype income:

- Completed deliveries

---

## Expense Sources

Prototype expenses:

- Upgrades
- Equipment improvements

---

# Starting Balance

The player should start with:

- Enough resources to begin
- Limited ability
- Clear reason to improve

The starting state should encourage growth.

---

# Delivery Reward Rules

Canonical reward design is defined in `03_Logistics/ORDERS.md`. The factors listed below are the Prototype v0.1 implementation scope of that canonical model.

Rewards should consider:

- Distance
- Difficulty
- Time requirement

---

## Example Reward Logic

Short delivery:

Low reward

↓

Longer delivery:

Higher reward

↓

Difficult delivery:

Premium reward

---

# Upgrade Balance

Upgrades should provide meaningful improvements.

---

## Upgrade Types

### Speed Upgrade

Effect:

Reduces delivery time.

---

### Capacity Upgrade

Effect:

Allows more opportunities.

---

### Efficiency Upgrade

Effect:

Improves company performance.

---

# Upgrade Cost Rules

Early upgrades:

- Affordable
- Frequent

Advanced upgrades:

- More expensive
- More impactful

---

# Progression Balance

Progression phases are derived from the canonical stage model in `01_GameDesign/PROGRESSION.md`. The phases below describe the Prototype v0.1 balance targets within Stage 1.

The player should experience:

Early game:

Fast progress

Middle game:

Strategic decisions

Late game:

Optimization

---

# Difficulty Scaling

Difficulty can increase through:

- Longer distances
- Higher expectations
- More complex orders

---

# Failure Balance

Failure design principles are defined in `01_GameDesign/GAMEPLAY.md` (Failure section). The Prototype v0.1 balance rule is: small penalty, no permanent setback.

Failure should teach the player.

Examples:

Delivery failed:

- Small penalty
- Opportunity to improve

Avoid:

- Destroying player progress

---

# Mobile Session Balance

The game should support short sessions.

Target:

A player should achieve something meaningful within a few minutes.

Examples:

- Complete a delivery
- Earn money
- Buy an upgrade

---

# Testing Rules

Balance should be tested by asking:

Can a new player understand progression?

Does every upgrade feel useful?

Is growth satisfying?

---

# MVP Balance Goals

Prototype v0.1 should achieve:

- Clear reward system
- Understandable upgrades
- Visible company growth

---

# Future Balance Expansion

Possible additions:

- Market changes
- Competitors
- Dynamic prices
- Regional differences

---

# Canonical Rule

Balance exists to support fun progression.

The player should always feel that improving the company is possible and rewarding.
