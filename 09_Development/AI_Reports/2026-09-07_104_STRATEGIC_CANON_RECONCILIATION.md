# Core Reconciliation Decisions

## Living-society identity

The game is now canonically described as a living economic and logistics society experienced from human scale.

The player's long-term path is no longer limited to always being the founder of one company. Compatible future roles include courier, specialist, employee, company member, founder, executive, investor, infrastructure operator, and multimodal professional.

The existing starter-company runtime remains a preserved compatibility foundation and will require explicit migration if/when future personal identity and company formation become separate authoritative concepts.

## Three-axis progression

Progression is now canonically governed by:

1. Personal Capability;
2. Company Capability;
3. World Access.

Money remains important but cannot substitute for all qualifications, people, infrastructure, authorizations, research, or operational requirements.

## Education/specialization

The canonical progression model now supports theory + practical qualification paths for terrestrial transport, maintenance, operations, business, drone, air, maritime, rail, and advanced technical fields.

The smartphone may host eligible theory; practical capability may require real in-world facilities, equipment, instructors, or supervised work.

## Business formation and competition

Company formation is now a progression event that may depend on entrepreneurship qualification, resources, local capacity/authorization, specialist capability, and an in-world civic/registry abstraction.

Multiple companies may compete for customers based on real simulated service performance, capacity, technology, value, reputation, coverage, and strategy.

## Equity/governance

Business Design preserves the owner-approved exploration baseline of:

- 51% internal/member pool;
- 49% external/non-member pool.

It also records member-share exit reconciliation, external portfolio separation, end-of-season dividend default target, founder historical identity vs executive control, governance safety, and Company Heart / Founder Artifact principles.

## Logistics scale

Strategic Logistics Design now governs one coherent custody/transport chain from walking courier through vehicles, warehouses, DronePorts, road/rail/air/sea multimodal hubs, international logistics, and very-late-game off-world expansion.

Advanced logistics requires the intersection of qualified people + valid equipment/vehicles + required infrastructure.

## World scale and server topology

Universe Design defines:

Neighborhood -> locality -> city -> region/county -> country/logical world -> international network -> frontier/off-world.

A player-facing country/world does not imply exactly one physical server.

Large worlds remain region-scoped/bounded in active rendering and high-frequency simulation.

## Multiplayer sequencing

The previous roadmap assumption that Multiplayer is only a final Phase 9 feature is superseded.

Stable identity and server-authoritative economy/company/persistence architecture must be prepared before real shared ownership, player employment, authoritative markets, shares, or contested infrastructure are enabled.

NPC/simulated participants remain valid so low-population worlds remain playable.

## Mobile-first correction

The stale Roadmap statement that the project is web-first was removed.

Installed Android is the primary owner/player quality surface.

`game-web/` remains the authoritative Phaser gameplay runtime.

`game-mobile/` is now correctly registered as a materially existing mobile shell.

Railway remains a secondary deployment/preview/smoke surface.

- Wave 3 — Personal Education, Professions and Legitimate Company Formation;

- Wave 6 — Server-Authoritative Online Society Foundation;
- Wave 7 — Regional and Multi-City Multimodal Logistics;
