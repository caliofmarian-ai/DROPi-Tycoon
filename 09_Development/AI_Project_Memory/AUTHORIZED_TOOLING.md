# DROPi Tycoon — Authorized External Design Tooling

## Purpose

This bootstrap companion records the Project Owner's standing authorization for AI agents to use connected paid external design capabilities when useful to DROPi Tycoon work.

The durable decision authority is `D-TOOLS-001` / `D-TOOLS-002` in `09_Development/AI_Project_Memory/DECISIONS.md`. Canonical domain documents remain authoritative for UI, creative, asset, legal and release truth.

## Authorized tools

### Figma

Authorized for in-scope work including:

- UI/UX exploration and review;
- mobile screen layouts;
- HUD, menus and interaction surfaces;
- reusable components and design-system work;
- prototypes and flow validation;
- visual specifications;
- supported design-to-implementation handoff.

### Canva

Authorized for in-scope work including:

- presentations and pitch material;
- campaign and social assets;
- promotional layouts;
- launch/store presentation material;
- communication-oriented visual production derived from approved project truth.

### Runway — FINAL PHASE ONLY

Runway is connected and Owner-authorized, but its credit-consuming capabilities are reserved for the **final production phase**.

Before the final phase:

- do not generate videos or images with Runway;
- do not run Runway edits, multi-shot jobs, upscaling, background isolation or other credit-consuming tasks;
- prefer Figma and Canva for design, iteration, prototypes, visual specifications and ordinary promotional exploration;
- any exception requires a new explicit Project Owner directive.

During the final phase, Runway may be used for:

- final cinematic teasers, trailers and launch-film production;
- final image-to-video and text-to-video sequences;
- final multi-shot promotional material;
- animation of approved final concept art or campaign stills;
- final video editing and visual cleanup;
- final reframing/expansion for delivery ratios;
- final image/video upscaling where needed;
- final background isolation/removal and compositing preparation where applicable.

Runway-generated material must preserve the project's evidence labels and truthful-claims boundary. Generated cinematic material is not evidence that gameplay, UI, world systems, economy, multiplayer, features, metrics, release status or player experience already exist in the runtime.

## Standing Owner authorization

Ordinary in-scope use of **Figma and Canva** does not require a new Project Owner approval each time.

Runway is already authorized for the final phase, but **must not consume credits before that phase** unless the Project Owner explicitly overrides this rule.

This standing authorization does **not** authorize:

- new gameplay, economy, architecture, account, monetization, legal, world or release truth;
- transfer of ownership between DT lanes;
- bypass of DT-13 legal/provenance requirements;
- bypass of DT-19 asset governance;
- bypass of DT-14 release evidence;
- bypass of DT-23 truthful creative/public-claim boundaries;
- treating an external design or generation workspace as repository-backed canonical authority.

## Authority rule

`FIGMA/CANVA/RUNWAY ARTIFACT != CANONICAL PROJECT AUTHORITY`

`NO RUNWAY CREDIT CONSUMPTION BEFORE FINAL PHASE`

GitHub repository-backed canonical documents and live GitHub remain authoritative. External artifacts intended to become implementation, asset, marketing or release evidence must enter the applicable repository, provenance, legal, asset, creative-claims and release workflows.

## Fresh-agent rule

A fresh DT agent that may benefit from design, communication or cinematic tooling should read `D-TOOLS-001`, `D-TOOLS-002` and the applicable canonical domain document before using Figma, Canva or Runway. Tool access is a capability; it does not expand the agent's ownership boundary.
