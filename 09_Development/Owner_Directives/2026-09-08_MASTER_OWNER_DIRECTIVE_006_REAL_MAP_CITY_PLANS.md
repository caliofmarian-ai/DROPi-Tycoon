# Owner Directive 006 — Real-map city plans and visual scale

Date: 2026-09-08. Source: direct owner conversation, tracked by #535.
Status: owner direction captured; Brăila implementation pilot under review.

The owner clarified that placing the existing fictional city at Romanian coordinates is insufficient: the city **plan itself must be inspired by the real map of Brăila**. Real geography should be recognizable within the established game visual style.

The worldwide starting target is one capital, four major cities and four smaller localities per country. Capital and major-city plans must be inspired by their real counterparts, including street structure, urban form and locally recognizable architectural character. This is a reusable worldwide direction, not a Brăila-only exception.

The visual journey must connect hero, active area, district, city, county, country region, country, continent and world. Geographic inspection must preserve the no-economic-teleportation rule in Directive 005. Country geography should show physical relief, rivers, lakes and settlement locations.

The owner also identified global, country and regional natural-resource inventory as a prerequisite for subsequent economy and transport work. A terrain color or a river outline is not such an inventory.

## Implementation and canonical ownership

Issue #535 owns the Brăila pilot and integration record in `04_World/City_Plans/README.md`. Existing canonical owners remain `04_World/LOCALITY_LOD_ARCHITECTURE.md`, `04_World/WORLD.md`, and economy/transport domain documents. This directive records the new instruction without rewriting their unrelated simulation rules.

The starting nine are a starting simulation/design target. The expanded regional locality catalog may contain additional real settlements. Source or special-status gaps must remain explicit; this request does not authorize invented capital roles or coordinates to fill nine slots. Final selection of each country's starting nine and detailed city production are separate from source locality ingestion.

The Brăila pilot preserves the legacy city/save identifiers for compatibility. It does not make Brăila Romania's national capital or claim the worldwide city rollout is complete.
