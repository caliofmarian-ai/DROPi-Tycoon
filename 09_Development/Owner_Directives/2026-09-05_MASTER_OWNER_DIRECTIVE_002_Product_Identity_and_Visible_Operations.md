# 1. Purpose

This directive records explicit Project Owner decisions made during physical Android owner review and visual-direction selection on 2026-09-05.

The directive does not itself redefine every gameplay domain. It provides authoritative strategic input that must be integrated deliberately into the correct canonical owners before implementation of affected future systems.

---

# 2. Game-First Product Identity

DROPi Tycoon must visibly present itself as a polished game, not as a technical prototype, browser wrapper, generic utility application, or developer tool.

The Project Owner approved the existing colorful DROPi Tycoon visual direction featuring:

- the DROPi Tycoon wordmark;
- a city/local-business backdrop;
- delivery-route/location-pin language;
- delivery drones;
- a parcel/logistics symbol;
- bright blue/cyan, green and gold accents;
- a friendly, optimistic tycoon-game presentation.

For the current adoption step, no further AI image generation is required or authorized. The already-generated, owner-approved images are to be preserved and used.

The selected identity must become the basis for the Android application icon, splash/loading presentation and future in-game visual language.

---

# 3. DROPi Is More Than Delivery

The owner reaffirmed that DROPi must not be represented as only a delivery company.

The broader ecosystem direction includes:

- local marketplace activity;
- local merchants and products;
- customers and community activity;
- multimodal delivery;
- drone operations;
- DronePort infrastructure;
- company and employee management;
- future shared economic/token concepts.

Delivery is an important operational subsystem, not the entire identity of DROPi.

DROPi Tycoon remains game-first. Ecosystem concepts must be translated into enjoyable gameplay rather than presented as advertising for the real application.

---

# 4. Visible Player and Delivery Progression

The current placeholder cube is temporary and must not remain the long-term visual representation of the player or delivery activity.

The Project Owner approved the following visible progression direction:

1. **On foot** — a visible person/courier moves through the world.
2. **Bicycle** — after purchasing a bicycle, the visible representation becomes a cyclist.
3. **Motorbike / scooter** — after purchasing the relevant vehicle, the courier visibly rides it.
4. **Car** — the owned car becomes the active visible delivery mode when selected/used.
5. **Van** — the owned van becomes the visible higher-capacity delivery mode when selected/used.
6. **Drone operations** — the human operator no longer physically travels with the parcel. The operator remains at an appropriate DROPi Port/control location while the drone itself becomes the moving delivery actor in the world.

Ownership must have visible consequences. Purchasing a transport capability should change what the player sees operating in the simulation.

This directive defines the visual/experiential intent. Detailed mechanics, costs, balancing, speed, capacity, vehicle selection and automation remain owned by their appropriate gameplay/logistics/economy documents.

---

# 5. Drone Operation Separation

Drone delivery must visually distinguish the human operator from the autonomous/remote delivery vehicle.

When a player is actively operating through a drone-capable setup:

- the player/operator is represented at a DROPi Port or appropriate control location;
- the drone leaves the facility and flies the delivery route;
- the parcel is visibly associated with the drone;
- the world should communicate that the operator is controlling/managing the operation rather than physically carrying the parcel.

The implementation must not simply replace a walking cube with a flying cube.

---

# 6. Visible Employees and Company Life

Hiring an employee must eventually create a visible person/activity consequence in the simulation.

Employees should not exist only as numbers, status strings or management cards.

Depending on role and current task, the simulation should be capable of showing employees:

- delivering;
- operating or monitoring drones;
- dispatching orders;
- sorting parcels;
- maintaining equipment;
- working inside operational facilities;
- participating in briefings/training;
- performing other role-appropriate work.

The employee-management UI remains important, but it should be connected to visible world activity.

Detailed AI scheduling, pathfinding, productivity and role mechanics require domain-specific implementation and are not activated solely by this directive.

---

# 7. DronePort Interior and Operational Visibility

Purchasing or operating a DronePort must eventually provide more than an exterior map marker.

The Project Owner wants a view into the facility and its operations.

The approved visual reference includes functional areas such as:

- control room;
- dispatch;
- parcel sorting;
- drone/equipment maintenance;
- briefing/training area;
- active staff;
- rooftop or dedicated launch/landing area.

The player should be able to understand that the DronePort is a functioning workplace and logistics node.

The exact floor plan, capacity, upgrade structure, staffing requirements and gameplay interactions remain subject to canonical DronePort/logistics design.

# 9. Audio Direction

The owner identified the complete absence of sound as a major product-experience gap.

DROPi Tycoon should gain an audio foundation including, at minimum over time:

- UI interaction feedback;
- order acceptance;
- successful and failed delivery feedback;
- money/reward feedback;
- hiring/onboarding feedback;
- warnings/errors;
- vehicle/drone activity;
- appropriate ambient/background audio.

Audio implementation is not part of the asset-import step unless separately scoped. This directive records it as an explicit product-experience requirement.

---

# 10. Shared DROPi Token Direction

The owner intends the future DROPi cryptocurrency/token to be a shared ecosystem element between DROPi Tycoon and the real DROPi platform.

This remains a strategic direction only at this stage.

It must not be implemented as an actual tradable, purchasable, cash-equivalent or real-world-value mechanic without separate:

- cross-project canonical alignment;
- game-economy design;
- technical architecture;
- legal/regulatory review as applicable;
- security review;
- explicit Project Owner approval of the concrete implementation.

Visual concept art may reference a future DROPi Token as ecosystem direction, but visual references do not create live financial functionality.
