# MASTER OWNER DIRECTIVE 003 — WORLD ART AND ASSET SYSTEM

Date: 2026-09-07
Project: DROPi Tycoon
Status: Owner-approved directive requiring canonical integration
Owner: Marian Caliof
Language: English

---

## 1. Decision

The Project Owner explicitly approves expansion of DROPi Tycoon from a small prototype-oriented asset set into a large, governed world-building asset library intended to support a living economic and logistics society.

The current approximately fifty candidate board crops are accepted as a useful first visual seed/reference set, but they are not considered remotely sufficient for the intended world.

The project must plan and execute toward thousands of coherent assets rather than a small fixed catalog.

---

## 2. Approved Art Direction

The owner approves the visual direction established by the current candidate asset set and authorizes quality improvement toward a richer three-dimensional appearance while preserving one coherent style from beginning to end.

Canonical description:

**Stylized 3D Pre-Rendered Mobile World — premium mobile tycoon presentation, soft-isometric / elevated 3/4 perspective, readable forms, controlled color, clear materials, consistent lighting, and production-quality visual coherence.**

This is not authorization to rewrite the current Phaser game as a full real-time 3D engine.

The runtime may continue to use optimized 2D sprite/texture representations of pre-rendered 3D-looking art where that best protects Android performance.

Rendering technology may evolve later under Technical Design, but the visual identity must not drift silently.

---

## 3. Diversity Requirement

Asset production must create real visual variety rather than recolored clones.

People must vary materially in:

- face and facial features;
- skin tone;
- hair and facial hair;
- age presentation;
- body silhouette and proportions;
- clothing;
- profession and equipment;
- role-specific posture/activity;
- recurring identity where the same person appears in multiple contexts.

The same principle applies to:

- vegetation;
- trees and seasonal/natural variants;
- animals;
- farms and agricultural environments;
- architecture;
- vehicles;
- companies;
- city forms;
- regional/environmental identity.

Diversity must remain stylistically coherent rather than mixing unrelated art styles.

---

## 4. Products Must Be Visible

DROPi Tycoon is fundamentally about producing, moving, storing, transferring, selling and delivering goods.

Products may not remain purely abstract economy labels when their physical form matters to gameplay.

The world must increasingly show:

- products;
- packaging;
- crates;
- parcels;
- pallets;
- containers;
- refrigerated/fragile/medical/industrial cargo classes where implemented;
- company inventories and handling contexts;
- goods moving through production, storage, transfer and delivery chains.

Companies should visibly produce, handle or sell recognizable categories of goods.

---

## 5. Economic World Requirement

Cities and regions must not be decorative maps only.

They may have distinct:

- population demand;
- industrial demand;
- production capability;
- resource availability;
- infrastructure requirements;
- business mix;
- growth needs;
- logistics dependencies.

Products and industries must connect to the existing canonical flow:

**materials/supplier -> production -> storage -> distribution -> merchant/marketplace -> customer**, with multimodal transfer where appropriate.

Visible art must support that economic truth.

---

## 6. World-Building Scope

The asset system must be capable of representing, over staged development:

- citizens, customers, employees, specialists and professions;
- animals, farms, rural environments and diverse nature;
- residential, commercial and industrial architecture;
- civic and special buildings;
- companies and industry-specific facilities;
- roads, highways and urban infrastructure;
- railways, stations, terminals, locomotives and freight/passenger trains;
- airports, air-cargo terminals, runways, aircraft and airport support assets;
- river ports, sea ports, docks, cranes, barges, river vessels, maritime ships and support craft;
- DronePorts, drones and drone-support infrastructure;
- warehouses and distribution centers;
- products, packaging and logistics props;
- city maps, regional/world map layers and transport gateways;
- multiple city archetypes with meaningfully different architecture and urban form.

Future late-game/off-world assets remain subject to staged implementation and runtime contracts.

---

## 7. Consistency Rule

Once the art direction is canonicalized, generated assets must not arbitrarily change style between categories or production batches.

The following must remain consistent unless the owner explicitly changes the art direction:

- perspective family;
- lighting logic;
- material treatment;
- degree of stylization;
- shape language;
- shadow softness;
- visual density;
- mobile readability;
- overall DROPi Tycoon color/identity language.

Variation belongs inside the world; style fragmentation does not.

---

## 8. Scale and Production Method

The owner approves a long-term library on the order of thousands of assets.

Production must use governed families and variants rather than random independent files.

Expected model:

**Master Family -> Controlled Variants -> Runtime-Specific Final Assets.**

Examples:

- human archetype -> identity/profession/outfit variants -> directional/animation runtime states;
- building family -> architecture/material/size/color variants -> footprint-specific runtime assets;
- product family -> product/packaging/cargo variants -> inventory/world/UI representations;
- city archetype -> modular architecture/environment/road kits -> composed districts and cities.

---

## 9. Performance Boundary

Visual ambition must not reverse the Android stability/performance recovery already achieved.

Rules:

- prefer reusable/cached textures and bounded active content;
- do not render an entire country/world at full fidelity every frame;
- do not introduce arbitrary giant textures where modular assets are appropriate;
- future large infrastructure asset dimensions must follow an implemented runtime footprint/camera contract rather than being guessed in advance;
- visual quality must be tested on the installed Android game.

---

## 10. Canonicalization Authorization

The owner explicitly authorizes creation of:

- `08_Assets/WORLD_ASSET_BIBLE.md` as the canonical visual/world-asset specialization;
- `08_Assets/MASTER_ASSET_PLAN_V2.md` as the governed production plan;
- corresponding updates to `08_Assets/ASSETS.md`, `07_UI/VISUAL_DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`, and `00_Project/DOCUMENT_INDEX.md` required to remove contradictions and establish discoverability;
- GitHub implementation issues/PRs required to execute the asset program.

This authorization applies specifically to the DROPi Tycoon world-art/asset domain. It does not automatically authorize unrelated future `BIBLE` documents.

---

## 11. Execution Rule

This directive is not satisfied by documentation alone.

After canonical integration, the project must execute staged production and runtime adoption.

The first executable production wave should establish:

1. art-style production contract;
2. human diversity families;
3. visible product/packaging families;
4. company/building foundations;
5. integration manifests and runtime-dimension contracts.

Later waves expand transport, infrastructure, nature, agriculture, cities and world-scale networks.

Candidate generation and owner visual review may happen ahead of runtime integration, but generated candidates do not become runtime-approved solely because they exist.

---

## Canonical Owner Rule

**DROPi Tycoon must grow into a visually coherent living economic world with real diversity, visible products, specialized companies, differentiated cities and multimodal infrastructure. The library may scale into thousands of assets, but every asset must remain part of one consistent visual language and one understandable economic/logistics society.**

---

End of Directive
