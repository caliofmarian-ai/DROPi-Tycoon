# Global Map Acceptance

Status: Execution/owner-review policy
Last Updated: 2026-09-08

Owner review is required only at meaningful visual/gameplay checkpoints, not for isolated cosmetic icons.

## Checkpoint A — Global Map
The player can open a real global map surface showing recognizable world geography, select countries, and see global-scale logistics/economy summaries.

## Checkpoint B — Country Drill-Down
The player can enter a country and see its capital, representative urban/rural nodes, external economic nodes and strategic transport/economy information.

## Checkpoint C — Local Integration
The player can enter a representative locality and reach a detailed playable scene while preserving company/player state and map breadcrumb context.

## Checkpoint D — Active Economy
The player can observe goods, producer demand, contracts, local price differences and logistics opportunities that arise from supply/demand rather than fixed values.

## Checkpoint E — Time/Evolution
Day/night and longer economic cycles visibly alter demand, traffic, production and world development.

Minor intermediate changes do not require owner interruption when CI and automated checks are green.
