# Global World Owner Decisions

Status: Binding owner-direction record
Last Updated: 2026-09-08

1. DROPi Tycoon is designed as a global-scale game.
2. All countries participate at the global layer.
3. The game uses sparse representative real localities rather than every city.
4. Capital + optional N/E/S/W urban + optional NE/SE/SW/NW rural/small nodes is the country-selection framework.
5. External factories/farms/industrial sites can exist outside localities.
6. Productive nodes create occasional jobs, contracts and later eligible acquisition opportunities.
7. Production chains, need, supply, demand, inventory and logistics drive the active economy.
8. World time includes day/night and long-term economic evolution.
9. Population, migration, tourism and specialists affect growth.
10. Countries/localities can grow, decline and found new settlements over time.
11. Advanced fictional macro events may create major migration, route disruption and state-status change.
12. Future national-currency strength may respond to simulated macro fundamentals.
13. Multiple persistent World Instances may operate in parallel.
14. New worlds start from baseline state; old worlds are not reset merely because a new world opens.
15. Logical worlds may span many physical servers/services.
16. New art is generated only when a concrete runtime need exists.
17. Owner manual verification is reserved for meaningful gameplay/visual checkpoints.
