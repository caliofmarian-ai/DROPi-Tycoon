# Representative Node Policy

Status: Canonical specialization
Last Updated: 2026-09-08

## Country locality roles

A normal country may expose at most one locality for each role:

- Capital
- Urban North
- Urban East
- Urban South
- Urban West
- Rural/Small North-East
- Rural/Small South-East
- Rural/Small South-West
- Rural/Small North-West

The capital is mandatory for a normal represented country. Other roles are optional.

## Selection priorities

Representative urban nodes should be chosen using a combination of:

- real geographic placement;
- regional coverage;
- population/importance;
- distinct economic specialization;
- logistics/infrastructure relevance;
- gameplay variety.

Representative rural/small nodes should be chosen using a combination of:

- diagonal geographic coverage;
- rural or small-town character;
- agriculture/resource/tourism/specialist economy;
- connectivity;
- gameplay variety.

## No forced symmetry

The N/E/S/W and diagonal roles are a sparse coverage framework, not a geometric requirement that every country have exactly nine nodes.

Microstates, archipelagos, elongated countries, city-states and unusual geographies may have fewer nodes or asymmetric placement.

## Real-world inspiration boundary

The selected settlement names/positions should be based on recognizable real geography, while simulated economy values, companies, prices and events remain game state.

## External nodes

Large farms, plants, mines, steelworks, ports and other external economic nodes do not consume locality-role slots.

## Canonical rule

**Use the smallest set of real representative localities that creates geographic, economic and logistics variety. Never add a fake city merely to complete the compass.**
