# World Instance Product Rules

Status: Canonical product specialization
Last Updated: 2026-09-08

- DROPi Tycoon may operate multiple persistent global World Instances in parallel.
- New worlds launch from a versioned baseline and evolve independently.
- Launching a new world does not reset/delete older worlds.
- Mature worlds remain valid historical economies unless a future explicit retention policy says otherwise.
- A logical World Instance may span many physical services/shards.
- Economy-bearing assets are isolated between worlds by default.
- Cross-world transfers of money, inventory, productive assets or infrastructure are forbidden unless separately designed and approved.
- Shared identity/achievements/cosmetic history may later cross worlds without importing economic power.
- Launch cadence is a live-operations decision informed by progression speed and population, not fixed today.
- New-world baselines use versioned geography/economy seeds so old-world history is not silently rewritten.

Canonical rule: **new worlds create fresh economic histories; they do not erase old ones.**
