# Building System

## Purpose

This document defines the building system of DROPi Tycoon.

Buildings represent the physical locations where customers, businesses, logistics operations, and company infrastructure exist.

Buildings connect the world simulation with economic and logistics gameplay.

---

# Building Philosophy

Buildings are not decorative objects.

Every important building should have a gameplay purpose.

Buildings can create:

- Delivery demand
- Business opportunities
- Operational advantages
- Expansion decisions

The world should feel functional and connected.

---

# Building Categories

Buildings are divided into several categories.

---

# Residential Buildings

## Purpose

Residential buildings generate individual customer demand.

Examples:

- Houses
- Apartments
- Residential complexes

---

## Gameplay Role

Residential areas create:

- Food deliveries
- Personal packages
- Consumer services

Characteristics:

- High customer volume
- Lower individual order value
- Frequent demand

---

# Commercial Buildings

## Purpose

Commercial buildings generate business activity.

Examples:

- Restaurants
- Shops
- Retail stores
- Small businesses

---

## Gameplay Role

Commercial buildings create:

- Regular deliveries
- Partnership opportunities
- Business contracts

Characteristics:

- Medium to high order frequency
- Stable revenue potential

---

# Industrial Buildings

## Purpose

Industrial buildings represent larger logistics opportunities.

Examples:

- Factories
- Production facilities
- Warehouses

---

## Gameplay Role

Industrial locations create:

- Large shipments
- Bulk logistics
- Future enterprise contracts

Characteristics:

- Lower frequency
- Higher value orders

---

# Company Buildings

## Purpose

Company buildings represent player-owned infrastructure.

Examples:

- Office
- Depot
- Warehouse
- Logistics center

---

## Gameplay Role

Company buildings provide:

- Storage
- Vehicle management
- Employee operations
- Expansion capability

---

# Special Buildings

Future special locations may include:

- Hospitals
- Universities
- Airports
- Government buildings
- Technology centers

These can create unique delivery opportunities.

---

# Building Attributes

Every building may contain:

## Location

The position on the map.

---

## Zone

The operational area where the building exists.

---

## Demand Level

The amount of activity generated.

---

## Importance

The strategic value of the building.

---

## Ownership

Possible ownership types:

- Public
- Customer owned
- Business owned
- Player owned

---

# Building Interaction

Players interact with buildings through:

- Deliveries

- Infrastructure development
