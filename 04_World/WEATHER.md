# Weather System

## Purpose

This document defines the weather simulation system of DROPi Tycoon.

Weather represents environmental conditions that influence the city, logistics operations, customer behavior, and company decisions.

---

# Weather Philosophy

Weather should create strategic situations, not random punishment.

The player should be able to adapt and make better decisions based on changing conditions.

Weather creates:

- Opportunities
- Challenges
- Operational changes

---

# Weather Categories

The world contains different weather conditions.

---

# Clear Weather

## Effects

Normal operating conditions.

Impact:

- Standard delivery speed
- Normal customer behavior
- Normal vehicle efficiency

---

# Rain

## Effects

Rain affects transportation efficiency.

Impact:

- Bicycle efficiency decreases
- Delivery time increases
- Customer expectations may change

Possible opportunities:

- Higher demand for certain services
- Increased value of reliable delivery

---

# Heavy Rain

## Effects

More severe operational impact.

Impact:

- Longer travel times
- Increased risk of delays
- Higher maintenance requirements

---

# Storms

## Effects

Extreme conditions.

Impact:

- Delivery restrictions
- Increased operational risk
- Possible temporary service limitations

Future drone operations may be affected significantly.

---

# Snow / Extreme Weather

## Effects

Advanced future weather condition.

Possible impact:

- Road difficulty
- Vehicle limitations
- Increased demand for essential deliveries

---

# Weather Impact On Logistics

Weather can affect:

## Travel Time

Poor conditions increase delivery duration.

---

## Vehicle Performance

Different vehicles react differently.

Examples:

Walking:

- Low impact

Bicycle:

- Higher impact

Van:

- Lower impact

Drone:

- Potentially high impact

---

## Operating Costs

Bad weather may increase:

- Energy usage
- Maintenance
- Employee requirements

---

# Weather Impact On Demand

Weather can influence customer behavior.

Examples:

Rain:

- More food delivery demand

Extreme weather:

- More essential delivery demand

Good weather:

- Normal activity

---

# Weather Forecast

Future versions may include forecasting.

Players could use forecasts for:

- Planning routes
- Scheduling employees
- Preparing resources

---

# Seasonal System

Future versions may introduce seasons.

Examples:

Spring:

- Normal operations

Summer:

- Different demand patterns

Autumn:

- Weather variability

Winter:

- Increased challenges

---

# MVP Weather Scope

The first playable version includes:

- Basic weather states
- Simple delivery modifiers
- Limited gameplay impact

Weather should enhance the simulation without creating unnecessary complexity.

---

# Future Expansion

Possible future systems:

- Realistic weather simulation
- Climate regions
- Weather prediction
- Emergency logistics
- Seasonal economies

---

# Balance Principles

Weather must:

- Create interesting decisions
- Reward preparation
- Avoid frustration
- Support the logistics theme

---

# Canonical Rule

Weather is an environmental factor that changes strategy, not a random obstacle.

Every weather mechanic must create meaningful gameplay.
