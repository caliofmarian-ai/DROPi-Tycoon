# Global World Reconciliation Changelog

## 2026-09-08 — Owner-approved global economy/world direction

Reconciled decisions:

- game scope is global;
- all countries are represented at the global layer;
- countries use sparse representative localities rather than every real settlement;
- external industrial/agricultural nodes may exist outside localities;
- delivery companies progress from occasional producer jobs to contracts and later eligible productive-asset ownership;
- supply, demand, inventory and logistics drive trade opportunity;
- products participate in production dependencies;
- world time includes day/night plus slower market/season/year cycles;
- cities/rural areas/countries can grow or decline over time;
- population, migration, tourism and specialist availability influence development;
- advanced simulation can found new settlements in eligible undeveloped territory;
- advanced fictional macro events can alter routes, population and country-state status;
- future national-currency behavior may be tied to fictionalized macroeconomic fundamentals;
- multiple persistent World Instances may run in parallel;
- new worlds start fresh from a versioned baseline without resetting old worlds;
- a logical world is not hard-bound to one physical server;
- owner review is reserved for meaningful visual/gameplay checkpoints rather than isolated cosmetic changes.

This entry records owner intent; detailed authority remains in the referenced canonical documents.
