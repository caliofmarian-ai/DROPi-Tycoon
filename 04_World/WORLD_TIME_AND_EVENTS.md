# World Time and Events

Status: Canonical specialization
Last Updated: 2026-09-08

## Time hierarchy

DROPi Tycoon uses multiple time scales:

- local clock — day/night and time-of-day behavior;
- operating day — routine deliveries, production, inventory and workforce cycles;
- week/market cycle — recurring contracts and short economic trends;
- season — agriculture, tourism, weather and broader demand changes;
- year/long cycle — demographics, construction, industrial development and national evolution.

Exact real-time conversion remains configuration/balance data.

## Day/night

Day/night can affect:

- lighting/ambience;
- traffic;
- business opening patterns;
- customer demand;
- industrial shifts;
- employee availability;
- delivery risk/capacity.

## Event scopes

Events can be:

- local;
- regional;
- national;
- global.

Events may influence supply, demand, route access, infrastructure, population, tourism, migration, specialist availability and prices.

## Event families

Examples include:

- festivals/tourism surges;
- harvest variation;
- weather/climate disruption;
- industrial opening/closure;
- infrastructure construction/failure;
- commodity shortage/surplus;
- financial cycles;
- workforce shortages;
- migration waves;
- border/route disruption;
- advanced geopolitical conflict/war as macro simulation.

War is not a tactical combat system. Its gameplay purpose is economic/logistics consequence such as route closure, migration, damaged capacity, emergency demand or changing state access.

Generated events inspired by real-world categories must not be presented as predictions or exact reenactments of current real conflicts.

## State evolution

Slow cycles may allow:

- population growth/decline;
- new construction;
- architectural diversification;
- industrial expansion/decline;
- new settlement founding where prerequisites exist;
- national prosperity/decline;
- political state status transitions in advanced fictional world instances.

## Canonical rule

**Time changes the world. Events modify the same economy/logistics state that normal gameplay uses; they must not form a disconnected random-event minigame.**
