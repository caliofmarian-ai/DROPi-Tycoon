## Owner acceptance

The owner approved the assembled V2 third-person film as the visual north star for DROPi Tycoon.

The intended runtime direction is to look and feel as close as technically practical to this film while preserving authoritative gameplay, truthful evidence and viable Android performance.

This approval does **not** change the film's truth classification. It remains `CINEMATIC / NON-GAMEPLAY — TARGET CANON` until exact runtime evidence qualifies captured footage as authentic gameplay.

## Creative objective

Show DROPi Tycoon as the game the owner intends to build: an immersive third-person open-world experience where the hero is physically present inside Brăila at human scale.

The viewer must immediately understand:

- this is a third-person game;
- the hero is a person in a city, not a miniature token;
- the city surrounds the hero with believable depth and scale;
- movement feels smooth and grounded;
- work, pickup and delivery happen through physical interaction with people and places;
- the experience can grow from one job into a larger economic life.

## Reference language

Owner shorthand: `GTA-like experience`.

Interpretation:

- third-person follow camera;
- human-scale city;
- believable street perspective;
- hero visible in-world;
- smooth walking/driving/traversal;
- spatially coherent entrances, vehicles, NPCs and destinations;
- cinematic but game-native camera language.

Not inherited:

- crime/gangster theme;
- GTA characters;
- GTA UI;
- GTA brands;
- GTA assets;
- GTA mission content;
- copied city or protected expression.

## Visual target

`GROUNDED STYLIZED REALISM`

Target qualities:

- realistic human proportions;
- full-height architecture;
- physically plausible street widths and sidewalks;
- perspective depth down streets;
- believable cars, vans, bicycles and street furniture;
- natural daylight and night-light options;
- materials that read as asphalt, concrete, brick, glass, metal, vegetation and water;
- Brăila identity through river/quay, streets, architecture and locality details;
- enough stylization/optimization to remain a game and support target hardware.

Forbidden look:

- Roblox-like blocky geometry;
- toy/minifigure hero;
- fixed isometric camera;
- miniature tycoon board;
- top-down diorama;
- chibi/cartoon proportions;
- fake photoreal live-action presented as gameplay.

## Camera contract

Primary lens feeling:

- third-person behind hero;
- camera approximately head/shoulder to several metres behind depending on traversal mode;
- mild elevation above hero eye level, not aerial;
- smooth follow and rotation;
- natural perspective, with readable horizon and street depth;
- camera may move closer for interaction and wider for vehicles;
- cinematic shots may temporarily detach, but must still look like the same 3D game world.

## Motion acceptance

The film FAILS if:

- walking visibly stutters;
- people slide instead of walk;
- camera jumps or jitters;
- animation cadence feels like low-frame-rate sprites;
- hero/world scale changes between shots;
- NPC locomotion feels robotic;
- interactions happen from implausible distance;
- buildings resemble blocks/toys;
- the camera rises into isometric presentation for normal local play.

Target delivery should appear at a stable cinematic/game frame cadence (normally 30 or 60 fps), with interpolation and animation authored for smooth motion.
