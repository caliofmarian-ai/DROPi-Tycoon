# Product Experience Principles

## Purpose

This document defines how DROPi Tycoon connects believable real-world logistics, entrepreneurship, human experience, entertainment, and the wider DROPi ecosystem.

DROPi Tycoon is a global game. It must remain complete, compelling, understandable, and enjoyable even for players who never use the real DROPi application and in places where DROPi does not operate.

The real DROPi platform may operate locally and expand gradually. The game must not depend on that rollout.

---

# Game First

DROPi Tycoon must succeed as a game before it succeeds as promotion, education, or training.

A player must be able to enjoy the complete game without using any real DROPi service or participating in any real commercial activity.

Promotion should emerge from familiarity, trust, curiosity, and a strong product experience rather than intrusive advertising.

---

# Real-World Inspiration

Every major gameplay system should be inspired by a recognizable real-world counterpart.

Examples include recruitment, onboarding, customer relationships, delivery operations, fleet ownership, maintenance, operating expenses, reputation, finance, warehouses, route planning, incidents, technology adoption, and business expansion.

Realism exists to make decisions meaningful, consequences understandable, and the company believable.

Realism should be simplified whenever full real-world complexity would reduce enjoyment, clarity, accessibility, or pacing.

---

# Human Simulation Principle

People in the simulation are not merely counters or production units.

Employees, candidates, customers, managers, partners, and other recurring people should increasingly be represented as understandable characters with identity and context appropriate to the game.

Where relevant, a character may have a name, visual identity, role, experience, skills, expectations, availability, performance history, professional development, satisfaction state, customer feedback, and career progression.

Human state should be driven by understandable gameplay causes and consequences rather than decorative numbers.

---

# Multi-Perspective Consequences

Important decisions should be considered from three perspectives where applicable:

## Individual

What changes for the employee, customer, courier, manager, or other person?

## Owner

What new opportunity, responsibility, cost, risk, or decision does the company owner gain?

## Company

How does the decision change capacity, cost structure, resilience, reputation, culture, growth, or operational complexity?

A hiring decision, for example, is simultaneously a change for the new employee, a new responsibility for the owner, and an increase in both capability and recurring obligations for the company.

---

# Cause and Effect

The game should teach through consequences rather than lectures.

Players should be able to understand why outcomes occur. Growth may increase both revenue opportunity and fixed costs. Training may cost resources before improving future performance. Better equipment may increase capability. Delivery outcomes may influence customer sentiment and reputation. Strong employees may become more valuable over time.

Numbers should support the story of the company rather than replace it.

---

# Entrepreneurial Fantasy

The player may begin with almost nothing and eventually build a large or global logistics company.

The game should support the fantasy of entrepreneurship, growth, wealth creation, innovation, and organizational leadership inside a fictional simulation.

Progress should feel earned through decisions, adaptation, risk management, and long-term development rather than meaningless numerical inflation.

---

# DROPi Ecosystem Bridge

DROPi Tycoon and the real DROPi platform are separate products with a shared conceptual ecosystem.

The relationship should follow this model:

```
Real-world inspiration
        ↓
Generic playable simulation
        ↓
Optional DROPi-specific education or training layer
```

The global game must not be tightly coupled to temporary operating procedures, service areas, prices, regulations, or workflows of the real company.

Those real-world details can change by location and over time.

---

# Learning Through Play

A player who spends time in DROPi Tycoon should naturally become familiar with concepts such as orders, pickup and delivery, routing, customers, couriers, vehicles, fleet capacity, employees, onboarding, operating costs, cash flow, reputation, reviews, growth, incidents, and operational trade-offs.

This learning should emerge through play. The main game must not become a mandatory training course.

---

# Future DROPi Training Layer

Selected mechanics may later support a separate DROPi Academy or training experience.

Training scenarios may cover real workflows such as a first delivery, pickup confirmation, customer communication, package handling, incident reporting, failed-delivery handling, safety procedures, or role-specific onboarding.

Real procedures and fictional simulation must remain clearly distinguishable when operational, legal, safety, or employment requirements are involved.

The training layer should be replaceable and updateable without rewriting the core game.

---

# Reality Boundary

The game may simulate extraordinary business success, wealth, and global expansion.

Fictional game outcomes must not be presented as guarantees, forecasts, or expected outcomes from participation in the real DROPi ecosystem.

The connection to reality should communicate genuine opportunities to learn, participate, work, build, or experiment where those opportunities exist.

---

# Product Experience Test

Before approving a major system, ask:

1. What happens in the comparable real-world situation?
2. Which parts create meaningful gameplay?
3. What human consequences should the player understand?
4. What owner decisions are created?
5. What changes for the company?
6. Can the system teach through cause and effect?
7. Is the result still fun, clear, and playable?
8. Could part of it later support optional DROPi education or training without coupling the global game to the real company?

---

# Canonical Rule

DROPi Tycoon must be a great global game first.

Its systems should be inspired by reality strongly enough to make the company, people, decisions, and consequences believable, while remaining simplified enough to be enjoyable.

The game may introduce players to the ideas behind the DROPi ecosystem, but its entertainment value and independence must never depend on the availability or use of the real DROPi platform.
