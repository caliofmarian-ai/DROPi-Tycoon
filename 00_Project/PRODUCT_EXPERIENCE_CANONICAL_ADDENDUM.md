# Product Experience Canonical Addendum

This addendum connects the existing Vision, UI, Assets, Architecture, and Roadmap rules into one implementation-facing direction.

## Global game independence

DROPi Tycoon is a global product. It must remain a complete and compelling game even where the real DROPi platform is unavailable.

## Reality-inspired systems

Major systems should begin from their real-world counterpart, then be simplified into understandable and enjoyable gameplay.

## Human-centered company simulation

People who matter to the company should increasingly be represented as recognizable characters rather than anonymous counters. Where the underlying systems support it, presentation may include visual identity, role, experience, status, progression, operational context, and meaningful feedback.

## Three-perspective design

Important company decisions should be evaluated for their effect on:

- the individual involved;
- the company owner or manager;
- the company as an organization.

## Learning through consequences

The game should teach logistics and entrepreneurship through visible cause and effect rather than through lectures.

## Optional DROPi bridge

The global game may later support a distinct education or training layer related to the real DROPi ecosystem. Real procedures must remain replaceable and separable from the fictional simulation.

## Visual quality

Prototype panels are not the permanent quality target. Player-facing systems should evolve toward attractive, coherent, characterful, mobile-first presentation while keeping gameplay state separate from rendering.

## Owner verification

Material player-facing changes should be reviewed on the deployed game. Functional acceptance and visual acceptance are separate decisions.

---

# Canonical Rule

Build DROPi Tycoon as an excellent game first, use reality to make its decisions believable, use presentation to make its company and people feel alive, and preserve a clean boundary between the global simulation and any future real DROPi training layer.
