# Overview

DROPi Tycoon is a next-generation logistics and business simulation game where players build a delivery company from the ground up and transform it into a global logistics empire.

Beginning as a single independent courier, players gradually expand their operations through strategic investments, infrastructure development, intelligent logistics, and advanced technologies such as autonomous drones and DronePorts.

The game combines deep management systems with accessible gameplay, creating an experience that rewards planning, creativity, and long-term thinking.

---

# Project Goals

The project has three primary objectives:

• Create an engaging and highly replayable logistics tycoon game.

• Design a scalable simulation capable of supporting years of future development.

• Serve as a virtual experimentation platform for logistics concepts inspired by the DROPi ecosystem.

---

# What Makes DROPi Tycoon Different

Unlike traditional tycoon games, DROPi Tycoon focuses on logistics as a living ecosystem.

Every system interacts with others.

Examples include:

- Customer demand
- Traffic
- Weather
- Infrastructure
- Technology
- Reputation
- Economy
- Artificial Intelligence

Every decision influences the future growth of the company.

---

# Player Progression

The player evolves through multiple business stages:

1. Independent Courier

2. Local Delivery Service

3. City Logistics Company

4. Regional Distribution Network

5. National Logistics Corporation

6. International Delivery Company

7. Autonomous Drone Operator

8. Global Logistics Empire

Each stage introduces new mechanics rather than simply increasing numerical values.

# Relationship with DROPi

DROPi Tycoon is an independent game inspired by the vision of the DROPi ecosystem.

Whenever possible, gameplay systems should be designed so that concepts, balancing ideas, algorithms, and logistics models can be reused or adapted for future DROPi products.

The game is not intended to replicate the real platform but to complement and inspire its future evolution.
