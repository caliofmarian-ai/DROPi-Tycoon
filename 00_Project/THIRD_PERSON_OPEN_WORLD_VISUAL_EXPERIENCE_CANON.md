# DROPi Tycoon — Third-Person Open-World Visual Experience Canon

Status: CANONICAL
Owner: Marian / caliofmarian-ai
Effective: 2026-09-12
Authority source: `09_Development/Owner_Directives/2026-09-12_MASTER_OWNER_DIRECTIVE_004_THIRD_PERSON_OPEN_WORLD_VISUAL_EXPERIENCE.md`

## Canonical statement

DROPi Tycoon local gameplay must present the hero inside a full-scale, immersive third-person 3D world.

The player should experience streets, buildings, NPCs, vehicles, parcels, shops, interiors and destinations at believable human scale, with perspective depth and smooth camera movement.

The intended experiential family is modern third-person open-world gameplay. GTA is a useful owner reference for camera/spatial presence only; DROPi Tycoon does not inherit GTA's crime theme, characters, art, UI, brands or protected expression.

## Required visual target

**Grounded stylized realism — human-scale third-person open-world presentation with believable proportions, architectural scale, street depth, readable materials, natural perspective and smooth player-follow camera.**

The world may be optimized and stylized for mobile/performance, but the local player experience must not read as:

- Roblox-like;
- blocky toy world;
- isometric diorama;
- miniature board game;
- top-down mobile tycoon map;
- chibi/miniature hero experience.

## Camera

Default local camera:

- third-person;
- behind/slightly above hero;
- hero visible;
- smooth follow;
- natural field of view;
- world horizon and street depth readable;
- no fixed soft-isometric camera as the primary local play mode.

Strategic maps remain separate and may be elevated/map-like.

## Scale

Canonical scale relationship:

`human hero < vehicle < house/shop < civic/industrial building < district < city`

The player must feel physically present inside that hierarchy.

## Marketing target

DT-23 creative must visually converge on the same experience:

`third-person hero -> work opportunity -> human-scale travel -> interaction -> pickup -> delivery -> result -> growth possibility`

Promotional material may be `CINEMATIC / NON-GAMEPLAY — TARGET CANON` until the runtime reaches this standard, but it must never be mislabeled as authentic gameplay.

## Superseded wording

Any older instruction that requires the main local gameplay experience to remain `soft-isometric / elevated 3/4` is `STALE` where it conflicts with this canon.

Those older documents remain useful for asset inventory/history until reconciled, but they no longer define the destination.
