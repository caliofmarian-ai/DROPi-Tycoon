# Drone System

## Purpose

This document defines the drone logistics system of DROPi Tycoon.

Drones represent an advanced transportation technology that allows the company to automate and optimize delivery operations.

The drone system is not available at the beginning of the game.

It represents a major technological milestone in company evolution.

---

# Drone Philosophy

Drones are not simple upgrades.

They represent a transition from traditional logistics to autonomous logistics.

Introducing drones creates new strategic decisions:

- Infrastructure investment
- Energy management
- Regulation
- Route planning
- Maintenance
- Operational efficiency

---

# Drone Unlock Progression

Drones become available only after the company reaches advanced development stages.

Requirements may include:

- Company reputation
- Research progress
- Financial capacity
- Infrastructure readiness

The player must build a strong foundation before accessing autonomous logistics.

Future versions may include different drone categories.

## Small Delivery Drone

Purpose:

Short-distance lightweight deliveries.

Characteristics:

- Low capacity
- High efficiency
- Urban operations

---

## Medium Delivery Drone

Purpose:

Professional logistics.

Characteristics:

- Increased capacity
- Longer range
- Higher operating cost

---

## Heavy Cargo Drone

Purpose:

Large-scale logistics.

Characteristics:

- High capacity

- Infrastructure requirements

# Drone Attributes

Each drone has specific characteristics.

---

## Capacity

Maximum package weight or volume.

Higher capacity allows larger deliveries.

---

## Range

Maximum operating distance.

Range determines possible delivery zones.

---

## Speed

Influences delivery time.

---

## Battery

Represents available energy.

Battery affects:

- Flight distance
- Operating cost
- Delivery planning

---

## Reliability

Determines operational stability.

Low reliability increases:

- Failures
- Maintenance needs
- Delivery risks

---

# Drone Operations

A drone delivery requires:

1. Available order
2. Suitable drone
3. Launch location
4. Valid route
5. Sufficient battery
6. Delivery completion

---

# Drone Limitations

Drones should not replace all traditional logistics.

They have limitations:

- Weather sensitivity
- Battery limitations
- Capacity restrictions
- Infrastructure requirements
- Regulations

Ground vehicles remain important.

---

# Drone Maintenance

Drones require:

- Repairs
- Battery replacement
- Software updates
- Technical supervision

Maintenance affects operational costs.

---

# Drone Technology Progression

Future research may improve:

- Battery efficiency

- Payload capacity
