# Vehicle System

## Purpose

This document defines the vehicle system of DROPi Tycoon.

Vehicles represent the transportation tools used by the company to complete deliveries.

The vehicle system connects logistics efficiency, company growth, operational costs, and strategic decisions.

---

# Vehicle Philosophy

Vehicles are not only transportation methods.

Each vehicle represents a business decision.

Players must consider:

- Purchase cost
- Operating cost
- Speed
- Capacity
- Reliability
- Maintenance
- Suitable delivery types

The best vehicle depends on the company's strategy.

---

# Vehicle Progression

Vehicle progression follows company evolution.

Early vehicles provide accessibility.

Advanced vehicles provide efficiency and scalability.

---

# Vehicle Categories

## Personal Transportation

Used during the early game.

Purpose:

Allow the player to perform deliveries personally.

Examples:

- Walking
- Bicycle

Advantages:

- Low cost
- No maintenance
- Easy access

Disadvantages:

- Limited speed
- Limited capacity

---

## Light Delivery Vehicles

Used when the company begins expanding.

Examples:

- Electric scooter
- Small motorcycle

Advantages:

- Faster deliveries
- Increased daily capacity

Disadvantages:

- Higher operating costs

---

## Delivery Vans

Used for professional logistics operations.

Advantages:

- High capacity
- Longer routes
- Business contracts

Disadvantages:

- Higher purchase cost
- Maintenance requirements

---

# Vehicle Attributes

Every vehicle has common attributes.

## Purchase Cost

Initial investment required to acquire the vehicle.

---

## Speed

Determines travel time.

Higher speed improves delivery efficiency.

---

## Capacity

Determines how many packages can be transported.

---

## Operating Cost

Continuous cost generated during usage.

Examples:

- Energy
- Fuel
- Maintenance

---

## Reliability

Represents the probability of operational problems.

Low reliability increases risk.

---

## Condition

Vehicle condition decreases through usage.

Poor condition affects:

- Speed
- Reliability
- Customer satisfaction

---

# Vehicle Maintenance

Vehicles require maintenance.

Maintenance prevents:

- Breakdowns
- Delivery delays
- Increased operating costs

Players must balance:

Repair expenses

versus

Vehicle replacement.

---

# Vehicle Assignment

Vehicles can be assigned to:

- Player
- Employees
- Future automated systems

Assignment affects operational efficiency.

---

# Vehicle Upgrades

Future versions may introduce upgrades.

Examples:

- Energy efficiency
