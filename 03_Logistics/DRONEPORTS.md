# DronePort System

## Purpose

This document defines the DronePort infrastructure system of DROPi Tycoon.

DronePorts represent the physical infrastructure required to operate a professional drone delivery network.

They are the foundation that allows the company to scale autonomous logistics.

---

# DronePort Philosophy

DronePorts are not simple charging stations.

They represent strategic logistics infrastructure.

A well-designed DronePort network provides:

- Better delivery coverage
- Faster drone operations
- Reduced downtime
- Increased automation capability

The player must carefully choose where and when to build them.

---

# DronePort Functions

A DronePort provides several essential functions.

---

## Drone Launch

Allows drones to start delivery missions.

Requirements:

- Available drone
- Valid route
- Sufficient battery

---

## Drone Landing

Provides safe arrival locations for returning drones.

---

## Battery Management

DronePorts manage energy operations.

Functions include:

- Battery charging
- Battery replacement
- Energy monitoring

---

## Maintenance

Advanced DronePorts provide technical support.

Functions:

- Repairs
- Diagnostics
- Software updates

---

# DronePort Levels

DronePorts can evolve through multiple levels.

---

## Level 1

Basic DronePort

Functions:

- Drone launch
- Basic charging

Purpose:

Small-scale operations.

---

## Level 2

Regional DronePort

Functions:

- Multiple drones
- Faster charging
- Basic maintenance

Purpose:

City-wide drone operations.

---

## Level 3

Advanced Logistics Hub

Functions:

- Large drone fleets
- Automated maintenance
- Advanced management systems

Purpose:

Professional autonomous logistics.

---

# DronePort Placement

Location is a strategic decision.

Important factors:

- Customer density
- Delivery demand
- Distance coverage
- Construction cost
- Future expansion potential

Poor placement reduces efficiency.

---

# Drone Network

Multiple DronePorts create a connected network.

A larger network provides:

- Greater coverage
- More delivery possibilities
- Higher automation

Network efficiency depends on:

- Location quality
- Infrastructure level
- Drone availability

---

# DronePort Costs

Building and operating DronePorts requires investment.

Costs include:

- Construction
- Energy
- Maintenance
- Upgrades

Players must evaluate long-term benefits.

---

# MVP Scope

The first playable version does not include active DronePorts.

DronePorts exist as future infrastructure planning.

The MVP focuses on traditional logistics.

---

# Future Expansion

Advanced DronePort systems may include:

- Automated battery swapping
- Weather protection
- AI traffic coordination
- Regional networks
- International drone corridors

---

# Balance Principles

DronePorts must:

- Require meaningful investment
- Create strategic advantages
- Support future automation
- Avoid making ground logistics obsolete

---

# Canonical Rule

DronePorts are the foundation of autonomous logistics.

They should represent a significant technological and business milestone in company evolution.
