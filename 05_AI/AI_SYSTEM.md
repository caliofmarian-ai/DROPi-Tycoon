# AI Systems

## Purpose

This document defines the artificial intelligence systems used inside DROPi Tycoon.

AI systems control simulation behavior, provide intelligent assistance, and enable future automation.

The purpose of AI is to create a deeper and more dynamic simulation while keeping the player in control.

---

# AI Design Philosophy

AI systems follow these principles:

## Intelligence With Purpose

Every AI system must create meaningful gameplay value.

Complex AI without player benefit should not be implemented.

---

## Player Control

AI can recommend, assist, and automate.

The player always remains responsible for major strategic decisions.

---

## Progressive Complexity

AI systems evolve with company growth.

Early game:

- Manual decisions
- Simple assistance

Late game:

- Advanced optimization
- Automation systems

---

# AI System Categories

DROPi Tycoon contains multiple AI system categories.

---

# World Simulation AI

## Purpose

Controls the behavior of the simulated world.

Responsibilities:

- Customer demand generation
- Business activity
- Population behavior
- Market changes

---

# Customer AI

## Purpose

Controls customer behavior and expectations.

Customer AI determines:

- Delivery requests
- Service preferences
- Satisfaction
- Loyalty

Factors considered:

- Delivery speed
- Price
- Reliability
- Company reputation

---

# Business AI

## Purpose

Controls business relationships.

Business AI manages:

- Partnership opportunities
- Contract generation
- Service requirements
- Business growth

---

# Logistics AI

## Purpose

Improves transportation operations.

Future capabilities:

- Route suggestions
- Delivery optimization
- Resource recommendations
- Fleet efficiency analysis

---

# Employee AI

## Purpose

Controls non-player employees.

Employee AI manages:

- Task execution
- Availability
- Performance
- Operational behavior

---

# Recommendation AI

## Purpose

Provides useful information to the player.

Examples:

- Recommended vehicle choice
- Profitable zones
- Operational warnings
- Expansion opportunities

Recommendations should explain their reasoning.

---

# Automation AI

## Purpose

Allows advanced companies to automate operations.

Possible systems:

- Automated dispatch
- Fleet management
- Drone operations
- Logistics optimization

Automation is unlocked gradually.

---

# AI Decision Model

AI decisions should consider:

- Current situation
- Available resources
- Company goals
- Economic consequences
- Environmental conditions

---

# AI Transparency

Important AI decisions should be understandable.

The player should know:

- Why a recommendation was made
- What factors influenced the decision
- What result is expected

---

# MVP AI Scope

The first playable version includes:

- Basic customer generation
- Simple world simulation
- Basic recommendations

Advanced automation is not required.

---

# Future Expansion

Possible future AI systems:

- Predictive demand analysis
- Smart city simulation
- Autonomous logistics networks
- AI company management
- Advanced drone intelligence

---

# Balance Principles

AI systems must:

- Improve strategic depth
- Support the player
- Avoid removing meaningful decisions
- Scale with gameplay progression

---

# Canonical Rule

AI systems exist to make the world smarter and more dynamic, not to replace the player's role as company leader.
