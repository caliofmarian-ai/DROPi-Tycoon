Natural Earth / world-atlas

The global strategic map and sparse global locality foundation use Natural Earth public-domain map data. The global country geometry is redistributed through world-atlas@2.0.2, derived from Natural Earth Admin 0 country boundaries at 1:110m.

Natural Earth Terms of Use · world-atlas source

Natural Earth attribution is not required by its public-domain terms; DROPi retains this credit and provenance for transparency.
