# Producer Logistics Contract Progression

Status: Executable planning specialization
Last Updated: 2026-09-08

## Stage 1 — Occasional jobs
A productive node may publish individual cargo jobs based on current inventory, input shortage or buyer demand.

## Stage 2 — Recurring contract
A logistics company with enough reliability, capacity, reputation and compatible transport can win a recurring service contract.

## Stage 3 — Dedicated logistics relationship
The company can reserve vehicles, staff, warehouse space and routes for a producer, earning higher predictable volume while accepting service obligations.

## Stage 4 — Strategic partnership
The logistics company may finance/operate supporting infrastructure, dedicated hubs or multimodal links.

## Stage 5 — Productive asset acquisition
A mature company may acquire eligible productive facilities subject to finance, governance, workforce, specialist and world rules.

## Stage 6 — Integrated producer/logistics operator
The company decides where to sell/ship output based on demand, price, transport cost, contracts, capacity, risk and inventory.

## Required invariants
- no passive output without inputs/time/capacity;
- no teleporting cargo;
- no contract volume beyond modeled capacity;
- no acquisition that bypasses critical-world fair access;
- no guaranteed profit merely from ownership;
- world supply/demand remains authoritative.
