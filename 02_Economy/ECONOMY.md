# Economy System

## Purpose

This document defines the fundamental economic simulation of DROPi Tycoon.

The economy represents the financial life of the company and connects all business activities.

Every decision made by the player has an economic consequence.

The goal of the economy system is to create meaningful business decisions while remaining accessible and understandable.

---

# Economy Philosophy

The economy of DROPi Tycoon follows one principle:

> Growth requires intelligent resource management.

Money is not the final objective.

Money is a tool used to:

- improve operations;
- purchase resources;
- expand the company;
- research technology;
- create new opportunities.

---

# Financial Resources

The main financial resource is:

## Company Money

Used for:

- Purchasing vehicles
- Paying salaries
- Maintaining equipment
- Building infrastructure
- Research
- Expansion

Future versions may introduce additional financial resources.

# Revenue System

Revenue is generated through company activities.

Main revenue sources:

- Individual deliveries
- Business contracts
- Service agreements
- Premium logistics
- Future automated logistics services

Revenue depends on:

- Number of deliveries
- Pricing
- Customer demand
- Company reputation
- Operational capacity

# Expense System

Every company activity creates costs.

Main expenses:

## Employee Costs

Includes:

- Salaries
- Training
- Benefits

---

## Vehicle Costs

Includes:

- Purchase price
- Maintenance
- Energy or fuel
- Repairs

---

## Infrastructure Costs

Includes:

- Offices
- Warehouses
- Future DronePorts

---

## Operational Costs

Includes:

- Software
- Communication

---

# Profit System

Profit represents the financial result after all expenses are deducted.

Formula:

Profit = Revenue - Expenses

A profitable company can reinvest and expand.

An unprofitable company must adapt its strategy.

---

# Cash Flow

Cash flow represents the movement of money over time.

A company may be profitable but still experience financial problems if it cannot maintain enough available cash.

Players must balance:

- Growth investments
- Current expenses
- Emergency reserves

---

# Company Growth

Company growth is determined by multiple factors.

Examples:

- Financial stability
- Reputation
- Infrastructure
- Fleet capacity
- Customer base
- Technology level

Money alone does not define success.

---

# Financial Risk

Rapid expansion creates risk.

Examples:

- Buying too many vehicles
- Hiring too many employees
- Expanding before demand exists
- Taking excessive loans

Smart companies grow at a sustainable pace.

---

# Economic Difficulty

The game should not punish experimentation.

Early failures should teach players.

Economic challenges should become deeper as the company grows.

A small courier company and a global logistics corporation should have different financial challenges.
