# Active World Market Addendum

Status: Canonical addendum to `02_Economy/MARKET.md`
Last Updated: 2026-09-08

The market is a layered world system rather than one static city table.

Canonical additions:

- every representative locality can have a local marketplace;
- regions and countries aggregate supply/demand rather than replacing local markets;
- goods may flow local -> regional -> national -> international and back through explicit logistics legs;
- prices may differ between localities because of supply, demand, inventory, distance, transport cost, congestion, currency/tariff abstractions and events;
- shortages create opportunities but essential categories require recovery/alternative supply paths;
- industrial buyers create upstream demand for inputs, not only consumer demand;
- tourist, migration and population shifts can alter product demand and workforce availability;
- seasonal agriculture and weather can alter supply;
- industrial openings/closures and infrastructure changes alter market structure over time;
- company contracts can reserve supply or transport capacity and therefore influence available spot-market volume;
- market information exposed to the player should be summarized by map layer and must not require full simulation of every inactive settlement.

Detailed production/trade rules are owned by `02_Economy/PRODUCTION_AND_TRADE.md`.

Canonical rule: **the marketplace reflects the current world state; it does not invent unlimited goods or fixed prices independently of production and logistics.**
