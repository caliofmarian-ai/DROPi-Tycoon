# Pricing System

## Purpose

This document defines how delivery prices are calculated in DROPi Tycoon.

The pricing system determines how much revenue the company receives for completing delivery orders.

The system must be simple enough for new players to understand while allowing deeper optimization in future versions.

---

# Pricing Philosophy

The price of a delivery should represent the value of the service provided.

Pricing should consider:

- Distance
- Delivery type
- Package characteristics
- Customer expectations
- Operational costs
- Service quality

The player should understand why prices change.

---

# Delivery Price Formula

The basic MVP pricing model:

Delivery Price = Base Price + Distance Cost + Service Modifier

---

# Base Price

Every delivery starts with a minimum service fee.

The base price covers:

- Order processing
- Basic operational costs
- Company service value

---

# Distance Cost

Longer deliveries require more resources.

Distance affects:

- Travel time
- Energy consumption
- Employee time
- Vehicle usage

Longer routes generate higher revenue.

---

# Service Modifiers

Additional factors may increase or decrease delivery value.

Examples:

## Priority Delivery

Higher price.

Requires faster completion.

---

## Large Package

Higher price.

Requires more capacity.

---

## Fragile Package

Higher price.

Requires careful handling.

---

## Business Contract

Custom pricing based on agreement.

Provides stable income.

---

# Customer Types and Pricing

Different customers value different services.

## Individual Customers

Focus:

- Speed
- Convenience
- Price

---

## Restaurants

Focus:

- Reliability
- Delivery volume
- Consistency

---

## Pharmacies

Focus:

- Speed
- Trust
- Availability

---

## Businesses

Focus:

- Scheduled logistics
- Professional service
- Long-term contracts

---

# Company Reputation Effect

Company reputation influences customer willingness to pay.

High reputation:

- More customers
- Better contracts
- Premium pricing opportunities

Low reputation:

- Reduced demand
- Lower customer tolerance
- Fewer opportunities

---

# Operational Cost Relationship

Pricing must always be evaluated against delivery cost.

A delivery that generates revenue but loses money is not successful.

Players must consider:

- Vehicle cost
- Employee cost
- Energy consumption
- Maintenance

---

# MVP Pricing Model

The first playable version uses:

- Fixed base price
- Distance calculation
- Simple package modifiers

No dynamic market pricing will exist initially.

---

# Future Expansion

Advanced pricing systems may include:

- Dynamic pricing
- Customer segmentation
- Competitor pricing
- Peak demand pricing
- Subscription plans
- Enterprise contracts
- Negotiation systems

---

# Balance Principles

Pricing must follow these rules:

- Customers should understand prices.
- Profitable strategies should be discoverable.
- No single pricing strategy should dominate forever.
- Prices should support company growth.

---

# Canonical Rule

The pricing system exists to create meaningful business decisions while remaining accessible.

Complexity should be introduced only when it creates additional strategic value.
