# Active World Economy Addendum

Status: Canonical addendum to `02_Economy/ECONOMY.md`
Last Updated: 2026-09-08

The company economy does not exist in isolation from the world economy.

Canonical additions:

- production sites consume inputs and create outputs over time;
- inventories constrain what can be sold or transported;
- locality/region/country demand can create shortages and surpluses;
- prices may vary by location according to supply, demand, inventory, transport cost and events;
- delivery companies can earn occasional producer jobs, then recurring contracts, then strategic partnerships;
- mature companies may acquire eligible farms, factories, plants, warehouses and other productive assets;
- owned production still requires inputs, specialists, workers, energy, infrastructure, maintenance and logistics;
- cities and rural localities can grow when their connected economy can sustain population, housing, food, energy, materials, employment and services;
- national prosperity/decline is an aggregate consequence of production, infrastructure, trade, workforce, population and world events rather than a single score;
- future national currencies may respond to fictionalized macroeconomic fundamentals but Company Money remains sufficient for normal gameplay until multi-currency settlement is separately activated.

Detailed rules are owned by `02_Economy/PRODUCTION_AND_TRADE.md`, `04_World/WORLD.md` and `06_Technical/WORLD_INSTANCES.md`.

Canonical rule: **world demand creates business opportunity; productive capacity creates supply; logistics connects the two.**
