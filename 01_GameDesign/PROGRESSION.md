# Player Progression

## Purpose

This document defines how players evolve throughout the game.

Progression is not measured only by money.

The player continuously unlocks new responsibilities, technologies, mechanics, and strategic possibilities.

Growth should always feel meaningful.

---

# Progression Philosophy

DROPi Tycoon follows horizontal and vertical progression.

Vertical progression increases the company's capabilities.

Horizontal progression unlocks new gameplay systems.

The player should constantly discover new ways to play rather than simply earning larger amounts of money.

---

# Company Evolution

Every company progresses through several development stages.

---

## Stage 1

Independent Courier

The player performs deliveries personally.

Available assets:

- Backpack
- Smartphone
- Bicycle

Main objective:

Learn the fundamentals of deliveries.

---

## Stage 2

Local Courier Service

Unlocks:

- Employee hiring
- Small office
- Multiple bicycles
- Basic statistics

The player begins managing people instead of performing every delivery.

---

## Stage 3

Local Delivery Company

Unlocks:

- Scooters
- Vans
- Fleet management
- Daily reports
- Maintenance system

The company starts operating independently.

---

## Stage 4

City Logistics Network

Unlocks:

- Warehouses
- Multiple districts
- Delivery zones
- Dispatch center

Operations become increasingly complex.

---

## Stage 5

Regional Logistics Operator

Unlocks:

- Long-distance deliveries
- Highway logistics
- Distribution hubs
- Regional contracts

Expansion becomes the primary objective.

---

## Stage 6

National Logistics Corporation

Unlocks:

- National map
- Large warehouses
- Business partnerships
- Government contracts

Management becomes increasingly strategic.

---

## Stage 7

Drone Logistics

Unlocks:

- Drone research
- DronePorts
- Flight planning
- Battery swapping
- Autonomous deliveries

Technology becomes a major competitive advantage.

---

## Stage 8

International Corporation

Unlocks:

- Airports
- Seaports
- Customs
- International regulations
- Overseas expansion

The logistics network becomes global.

---

## Stage 9

Global Logistics Empire

Unlocks:

- AI management
- Predictive logistics
- Automated distribution
- Global optimization
- Worldwide DronePort network

The player controls a fully autonomous logistics ecosystem.

---

# Progression Systems

Player progression is driven by multiple systems simultaneously.

Examples include:

- Revenue
- Reputation
- Technology
- Infrastructure
- Fleet size
- Employee count
- Customer satisfaction
- Research

No single number determines progress.

---

# Unlock Philosophy

New mechanics should never appear too early.

Players must first master existing systems before discovering additional complexity.

Every unlock should solve existing problems while introducing new strategic decisions.

---

# Learning Curve

The game teaches through gameplay.

Instead of long tutorials, players naturally discover mechanics as the company grows.

Learning should feel rewarding rather than instructional.

---

# Endless Progression

There is no maximum company size.

After reaching the highest progression stage, players continue optimizing:

- Efficiency
- Sustainability
- Automation
- Expansion
- Innovation
- Profitability

The game remains engaging through continuous improvement.

---

# Canonical Rule

Every future gameplay system must integrate naturally into this progression model.

New mechanics should expand existing gameplay instead of replacing previous systems.
