# Education, Specialization and Group Capability

## Purpose

This document specializes `01_GameDesign/PROGRESSION.md` and the global-world economy canon for education, professions, specialist availability, research and cooperative company progression.

Education is not decorative lore. It is a world-embedded progression system that creates real personal and organizational capability.

---

# 1. Core Principle

A player, employee, NPC specialist or company cannot perform an advanced action merely because enough money exists.

Capability can require a legitimate combination of:

- prerequisite knowledge;
- theory;
- practical training;
- supervised experience;
- assessment;
- authorization/certification abstraction;
- suitable equipment;
- suitable facility;
- instructor/assessor/supervisor;
- time;
- money;
- location;
- company membership or employment where relevant.

The trained person's capability and the organization's capability are distinct.

---

# 2. Personal Capability

Qualifications belong to a person unless explicitly defined as corporate technology/research.

Personal capability may include:

- occupation/profession family;
- specialization;
- theory completion;
- practical milestones;
- supervised experience;
- assessment result;
- qualification status;
- renewal/expiry where later useful;
- equipment/facility familiarity;
- reputation/track record where relevant.

Bankruptcy or company failure does not erase legitimately earned personal knowledge/qualification unless a separate expiry/renewal mechanic applies.

---

# 3. Company Capability

A company can operate a capability only when it controls the required combination of:

- qualified people;
- facilities;
- equipment;
- infrastructure;
- materials/inventory;
- utilities;
- money/cash flow;
- authorization/access;
- technology/research;
- operating capacity.

A company does not permanently own the personal qualification of an employee.

If a required specialist leaves, becomes unavailable or changes role, the company may lose or reduce the affected capability until replacement capacity exists.

---

# 4. Geographic Education Network

Schools, vocational centers, universities, academies, specialist institutes, company training centers, laboratories and research facilities are real economic/world nodes.

Programs can have geographic availability.

A locality may therefore have:

- no relevant program;
- basic vocational capacity;
- one specialist program;
- a regional university/training hub;
- a national center of excellence;
- a rare international specialization.

This geography creates travel, housing, tuition, absence-from-work and migration decisions.

---

# 5. Real-World-Inspired Institution Research

When the game uses a real-world-inspired school, university, academy, training center or program as factual baseline inspiration, the record must be source-backed.

Required research metadata should include where practical:

- institution stable identity;
- institution name;
- city/locality;
- country;
- program/specialization area;
- program level/type;
- relevant facility/equipment where material;
- source URL/reference;
- source publisher;
- source date/verification date;
- factual-confidence or missing-data status.

Do not claim exact legal qualification equivalence merely because a real institution teaches a related subject.

Gameplay may use a clearly fictionalized/simplified training contract inspired by that real educational geography.

---

# 6. Training Lifecycle

A typical qualification lifecycle may be:

`ELIGIBLE -> ENROLLED -> THEORY_IN_PROGRESS -> PRACTICAL_IN_PROGRESS -> SUPERVISED_EXPERIENCE -> ASSESSMENT_READY -> QUALIFIED`

Alternative shorter paths are valid for basic skills.

Failure, withdrawal, interrupted study and later resumption may exist where useful.

Completion must be exactly-once and cannot duplicate qualifications, money or progression receipts.

---

# 7. Theory and Remote Learning

Some theory may be available through the in-world smartphone, company learning system or remote study.

Remote theory does not automatically grant physical operating capability.

Examples of theory-friendly domains:

- route/road theory;
- business/finance theory;
- procedural/safety theory;
- introductory logistics;
- drone theory;
- technical principles;
- company procedures;
- research methods.

Practical capability remains gated where the real gameplay activity requires practice/equipment/supervision.

---

# 8. Practical Training

Practical training can require location-specific assets such as:

- vehicle;
- workshop;
- warehouse;
- farm;
- factory;
- ship/port;
- rail terminal/vehicle;
- airport/aircraft/simulator;
- DronePort/drone;
- laboratory;
- industrial plant;
- specialized machine.

Training should create useful work-like gameplay rather than only a timer when practical interaction can be modeled safely and economically.

---

# 9. Profession Families

The profession catalog is extensible and should be driven by world/economic needs.

Families include, without limitation:

- logistics and delivery;
- road transport;
- warehouse/dispatch;
- maintenance/mechanics;
- construction;
- agriculture;
- forestry;
- mining/extraction;
- manufacturing/processing;
- utilities/energy;
- maritime/fluvial;
- rail;
- aviation;
- drone operations;
- engineering/technical roles;
- business/finance/procurement;
- healthcare/service roles where modeled;
- education/training;
- research/technology;
- management/operations.

Do not populate every locality with every profession. Profession availability follows the actual local economy, institutions and infrastructure.

---

# 10. Specialist Demand

Specialist demand is caused by operating requirements.

Examples:

- port expansion creates maritime/port/engineering demand;
- industrial plant creates operators, maintenance and engineering demand;
- rail terminal creates rail/terminal specialists;
- research facility creates scientific/technical roles;
- farm expansion creates agricultural and machinery-maintenance roles;
- energy infrastructure creates technical and operational demand.

A company posting or mission must not invent a specialist need with no underlying facility/project/operation.

---

# 11. Cooperative Education Sponsorship

A group/company may sponsor one member's education.

A sponsorship contract can define:

- student/member;
- qualification target;
- institution/program;
- start/completion conditions;
- tuition/training cost;
- travel/housing cost where relevant;
- Personal Money contributors;
- Company Money contribution where authorized;
- time commitment;
- work-absence consequence;
- completion receipt;
- optional employment/service commitment if later canonized.

Multiple players may contribute governed funds to one education objective.

All transfers preserve ownership and money conservation.

---

# 12. Group Capability and Team Composition

Some projects require a team rather than one universal character.

A project may require, for example:

- project/operations lead;
- mechanical specialist;
- electrical/automation specialist;
- qualified operators;
- logistics capacity;
- safety/quality capability where modeled;
- financing;
- materials;
- site/infrastructure.

The system should check the actual group's available specialist capacity.

This creates strategic cooperation: players can choose complementary careers rather than all following one optimal skill tree.

---

# 13. Internal Mobility and External Recruitment

When a company lacks a specialist, valid strategies may include:

- train an existing member;
- sponsor a member externally;
- recruit an NPC specialist;
- recruit a human player later in multiplayer;
- contract an external company/provider;
- relocate a specialist;
- partner with another organization;
- postpone the project.

Money alone is not guaranteed to solve a specialist shortage instantly.

---

# 14. Education as an Economic Sector

Education institutions consume resources and create economic effects.

They may require:

- teachers/instructors;
- facilities;
- equipment;
- maintenance;
- utilities;
- student housing/transport;
- supplies;
- research funding.

They create:

- qualified workforce;
- migration/student movement;
- local service demand;
- research capacity;
- company partnerships;
- future settlement attractiveness/development.

An education hub can therefore become a real economic advantage for a city or region.

---

# 15. Research and Technology

Research is a later advanced capability connected to institutions, specialists, facilities, funding, time and evidence/progress.

Research may unlock or improve:

- production processes;
- automation;
- logistics efficiency;
- energy systems;
- materials;
- infrastructure;
- vehicles/drones;
- organizational processes;
- future advanced technologies.

Research cannot be a pure money-to-upgrade button when specialist/facility requirements are relevant.

Corporate technology belongs to the organization or project according to the relevant ownership contract; personal research qualifications remain personal capability.

---

# 16. Migration for Education

A player may need to travel or temporarily relocate for study.

Possible costs/consequences include:

- transport;
- housing;
- tuition;
- reduced working time;
- temporary absence from company operations;
- visa/permission abstractions where later useful;
- new social/economic opportunity in the destination locality.

Completion may allow the player to return home, remain in the new locality or migrate elsewhere.

Education therefore participates in the same world-travel and migration system as employment and company expansion.

---

# 17. Settlement and Country Development Effects

Education capacity influences long-term development through:

`institution -> specialist supply -> company capability -> production/services/research -> jobs/investment -> settlement attractiveness -> population/demand -> further development`

A locality that gains a training/research institution may become a regional specialist hub.

A locality that lacks required training may depend on migration, external recruitment or partnerships.

---

# 18. User Experience Rule

The player-facing system must clearly explain:

- what capability is missing;
- where it can be learned;
- what prerequisites apply;
- what it costs;
- how long it takes;
- what practical training is required;
- who can sponsor it;
- what new activities it enables.

Do not expose opaque lock icons with no causal explanation.

---

# Canonical Rule

**Knowledge and specialist capacity are economic infrastructure. Players and companies progress by learning, practicing, cooperating, sponsoring specialists and accessing the right institutions; advanced organizations require the right people as well as money, equipment and facilities; and education/research geography creates real travel, migration, trade and development decisions.**
