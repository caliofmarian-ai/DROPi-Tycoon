## Precedence

For the recovery-first campaign, this amendment supersedes the old opening assumption in `PLAYER_SMARTPHONE.md` that the hero already owns a smartphone at the start.

The smartphone remains the canonical **portable interface once it is legitimately owned**. It is no longer a free starting possession.

The phone does not own map truth, demand, marketplace inventory, money, cargo, company formation, multiplayer membership or legal status. It projects information and requests actions from those authorities.

## Narrative purpose

The first smartphone is a meaningful earned asset.

The hero begins without one and learns why it matters by doing real work in a world where merchants, recipients and businesses are already using phones for maps, tracking, confirmation and communication.

One early Maria-chain delivery should visit a phone/electronics merchant or equivalent legitimate business. The hero sees delivery tracking/confirmation on other people's devices and forms the need:

> Everybody here is connected. They can see where things are going, confirm deliveries, find businesses. If I ever want to do this seriously, I need a phone of my own.

This creates a narrative fact/opportunity. It does not grant an item.

The first phone is purchased later with real Personal Money after the founder aspiration has formed. The purchase occurs through a legitimate physical merchant/asset path because the hero does not yet have the phone marketplace.

## Stage 0 — `NO_PHONE`

Hero owns no personal smartphone.

Available:

- ordinary third-person world view;
- physical signs/landmarks;
- verbal/printed delivery instructions;
- ordinary mission/objective presentation that does not pretend to be phone GPS;
- direct human dialogue;
- merchant-side/customer-side tracking that the hero can observe but not personally operate.

Unavailable:

- player phone overlay;
- personal electronic minimap/GPS;
- client feed;
- delivery marketplace;
- company operations;
- partner/cofounder discovery;
- remote asset marketplace.

The first deliveries therefore feel harder and more local. The friction is deliberate but must remain playable.

## Stage 1 — `BASIC_PERSONAL_PHONE`

Requires authoritative ownership of the purchased handset.

The phone becomes a physical object: the hero takes it from a pocket/bag, looks at it, the screen becomes readable, and closing it returns to the exact world state.

Allowed surfaces:

### Nearby Map / GPS

- current position;
- small surrounding area;
- simple route/heading help;
- public points of interest sourced from governed world/locality data;
- visible business categories such as shops, commercial premises, farms, factories/plants, accommodation, institutions and other public economic locations.

Not yet visible merely because the phone exists:

- delivery clients;
- private customer demand;
- partner carriers;
- contract feed;
- company dispatch;
- local marketplace purchasing;
- human cofounder/member discovery;
- full city/regional/world economic map.

The phone can show that a factory exists. It cannot claim that the factory is the player's client until an authoritative business relationship/opportunity exists.

## Stage 2 — `REGISTERED_STARTER_ENTITY`

Requires the first legitimate jurisdiction-governed business entity.

The entity formation event does not magically create demand; it authorizes business-facing surfaces to expose demand that actually exists and for which the company is eligible.

New phone capabilities may include:

- eligible local delivery-client/opportunity feed;
- wider locality/city map coverage;
- customer/business relationship information;
- company account/status;
- order/contract tracking;
- business communications;
- **local marketplace browsing**;
- eligible listings for assets, equipment and goods produced/offered by the surrounding economy.

Marketplace browsing does not teleport goods or capability. Purchase settlement, ownership transfer, physical custody, delivery and required qualifications remain authoritative system responsibilities.

## Stage 3 — `OPERATIONAL_COMPANY`

Requires appropriate company capability, not merely registration.

Progressively unlocked phone surfaces may include:

- richer dispatch;
- workforce schedules;
- vehicle/fleet status;
- wider client intelligence where legitimate;
- training/study surfaces;
- local analytics;
- supplier/customer relationship information;
- marketplace filters relevant to the company's actual capabilities.

Physical actions remain physical when required.

## Stage 4 — `HUMAN_COMPANY`

Requires the governed real-player membership gate from `EARLY_COMPANY_SOCIAL_SCALING_CONTRACT.md`.

Additional surfaces may include:

- cofounder/member communication;
- shared dispatch/team state;
- partner/company discovery;
- shared company roles/permissions;
- company-level planning;
- broader contract visibility when company capability supports it.

NPCs cannot unlock human-company communication/member surfaces by substituting for real players.

## Stage 5 — `REGIONAL_NETWORK`

Requires real route/capability/coverage authority.

May unlock:

- region-scale map;
- inter-city route information;
- regional customers/suppliers;
- regional marketplace visibility;
- multimodal status where qualified/authorized.

## Stage 6 — `GLOBAL_NETWORK`

Requires earned international/world-network capability.

May unlock:

- country/world semantic map;
- international company/partner surfaces;
- cross-border marketplace visibility;
- multimodal international planning;
- global network status.

A registered local micro-business cannot reveal every private commercial opportunity on Earth merely because it owns a phone.

## Map visibility rule

Map scale and business information are separate gates.

The player may know a place exists without being eligible to work for it.

Example:

- BASIC phone: sees a factory icon/public category nearby;
- REGISTERED entity: may see that factory as an eligible customer only if real demand/opportunity exists;
- later capability: sees relevant contracts/routes if the company can actually serve them.

## First phone asset rule

The first smartphone is intended to be the first significant business-enabling personal asset after the founder thought.

Suggested causal chain:

`early delivery -> observe phone tracking -> phone need realized -> founder thought -> save Personal Money -> physically buy basic phone -> nearby map opens -> form legal entity -> business apps unlock`.

Exact handset price/model is balance/economy data, not narrative canon.

## Physical presentation

Opening the phone must be visible as an action:

1. hero reaches pocket/bag;
2. phone appears in hand;
3. short glance/hold animation;
4. screen becomes readable;
5. player navigates apps;
6. close action returns screen to world view;
7. hero puts phone away.

This animation contract is reusable for Male/Female heroes and later character variants.

## Locked-function UX

Locked functions should communicate prerequisites instead of simply disappearing where that helps player understanding.

Examples:

- `Delivery Clients — register a business first`;
- `Marketplace — business account required`;
- `Regional Map — establish regional operating capability`;
- `Company Members — join or form a human company`.

Do not expose technical IDs or false promises.

## Global portability

The phone is global infrastructure but its data is locality/world-instance scoped.

Different countries may expose different legal/business services. The interface architecture is reusable; the underlying legal and economic truth is not copied from Brăila.

## Canonical rule

**The smartphone is the earned portable gateway through which DROPi Tycoon's complexity becomes visible. Before ownership the hero experiences the city physically. A basic phone reveals nearby public world information. A legitimate business entity unlocks clients, wider business mapping and local marketplace surfaces. Company, human-social, regional and global functions open only when the authoritative game state earns them.**
