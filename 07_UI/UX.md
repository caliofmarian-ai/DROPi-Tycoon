# User Experience System

## Purpose

This document defines the User Experience (UX) principles of DROPi Tycoon.

UX describes how the player interacts with the game, learns its systems, makes decisions, and experiences company growth.

---

# UX Philosophy

DROPi Tycoon should create the feeling of building and managing a growing logistics company.

The player should feel:

- Progress
- Control
- Discovery
- Strategic ownership

The game should be accessible at the beginning while allowing deeper management later.

---

# Core UX Principles

## Easy To Learn

The first interactions should be simple and understandable.

The player should quickly understand:

- What to do
- Why it matters
- How to improve

---

## Depth Through Progression

Complexity should be introduced gradually.

Early game:

- Basic deliveries
- Simple decisions

Later game:

- Employees
- Vehicles
- Expansion
- Automation

---

## Meaningful Decisions

The player should regularly make strategic choices.

Examples:

- Which orders to accept
- Which vehicle to buy
- Which zone to expand into

---

# Player Journey

## Beginning Phase

The player starts with limited resources.

Focus:

- Learning the delivery system
- Completing first orders
- Understanding income and expenses

---

## Growth Phase

The company begins expanding.

New systems introduced:

- Vehicles
- Employees
- Business relationships

---

## Advanced Phase

The player manages a larger organization.

Systems include:

- Multiple zones
- Automation
- Drone logistics
- Advanced optimization

---

# Tutorial Experience

The tutorial should teach through gameplay.

The player learns by doing.

Initial tutorial concepts:

- Accepting an order
- Completing a delivery
- Earning money
- Improving operations

Avoid overwhelming the player with information.

---

# Feedback Systems

The game should constantly communicate results.

Examples:

Positive feedback:

- Successful delivery
- Company growth
- New opportunities

Negative feedback:

- Delays
- High costs
- Customer dissatisfaction

Feedback should help the player improve.

---

# Player Motivation

The game motivates through:

## Growth

Starting small and becoming a major logistics company.

---

## Achievement

Unlocking:

- New vehicles
- New zones
- New technologies

---

## Optimization

Improving efficiency and making better decisions.

---

# Complexity Management

New mechanics should be introduced only when they provide value.

Avoid:

- Unnecessary menus
- Excessive information
- Confusing systems

---

# Accessibility

The game should support different player styles.

Examples:

Casual players:

- Simple management

Advanced players:

- Detailed optimization

---

# MVP UX Scope

The first playable version includes:

- Clear introduction
- Simple delivery loop
- Basic company progression
- Understandable feedback

---

# Future Expansion

Possible UX improvements:

- AI assistant
- Advanced tutorials
- Personalized recommendations
- Management shortcuts

---

# UX Success Criteria

The player should always understand:

- Current situation
- Available options
- Consequences of decisions
- How to improve

---

# Canonical Rule

Good UX transforms complex simulation into an enjoyable strategic experience.

The player should feel like a company owner, not a system operator.
